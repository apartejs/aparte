## What comes with it

- **Messages that stream as they are written**: the [response](/components/response/), the
  model's [reasoning](/components/reasoning/), [code](/components/code-block/),
  [tool calls](/components/tool/), [errors](/components/error/), and
  [views of your own](/components/custom-view/). Markdown and code highlighting come from two
  plugins, [Marked](/plugins/marked/) and [Shiki](/plugins/shiki/).
- **Tools, with a person in the loop**: the model calls your functions, the ones you choose
  wait for the person's [approval](/guides/tools/), and the model can
  [ask a question](/guides/elicitation/) in the middle of a turn.
- **Conversations**: [retry](/guides/conversations-branching/) keeps each answer as a version you
  can step through, edit rewrites a message and its reply — both one line to turn on — with a
  [conversation list](/components/conversation-list/), [attachments](/guides/attachments/) when
  you ask for them, and [storage](/guides/conversation-persistence/) you plug in.
- **Your look**: colours, sizes and shapes are [CSS variables](/guides/theming/), and a
  [kit of classes](/kit/) draws the controls around the chat.
- **Any model**: [OpenAI-compatible APIs](/providers/ai/openai-compat/), the
  [Vercel AI SDK](/providers/ai/ai-sdk/), a [model in the browser](/providers/ai/transformers/),
  or an agent that runs its own loop, over [AG-UI](/providers/agent/ag-ui/).
- **Any framework**: [React, Vue, Svelte, Angular](/frameworks/), or none.

<a href="/components/">{COMPONENTS.total} components</a> in all, each on a page of its own with
live examples.

## What it leaves to you

Routing, sign-in, settings, and where conversations are kept belong to your app. aparté draws
the chat and runs the conversation; storage is an interface you implement over your own
database, IndexedDB or API.

## What you need

- A recent browser: the [support matrix](/reference/support/) has the exact versions.
- npm and a bundler, or one script tag from a CDN.
- Node 18 or later, for the parts that run on your server.
- A model: the scripted one to start, then the person's own key, a model on their machine, or
  your server.

## Where it stands

aparté is in beta. Its public surface — elements, attributes, events, exports — is
[frozen](/reference/stability/), and every release is in the [changelog](/changelog/).

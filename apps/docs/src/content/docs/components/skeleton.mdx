`.aparte-skeleton` is a block that pulses where text, a picture or a card will be. It has no
margin of its own: lay a group out the way the real content will be (a grid with a gap), and the
skeleton takes the same shape. The [conversation list](/components/conversation-list/) and the
transcript draw their loading state with it.

## Accessibility

- A skeleton is decoration: give it `aria-hidden="true"`, or put the group in one region marked
  `aria-busy` with a line that says what is loading, so a screen reader does not read empty boxes.
- The pulse stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

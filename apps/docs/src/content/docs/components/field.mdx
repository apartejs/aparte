Every form control a settings screen needs, drawn on the browser's own elements. `.aparte-field`
is one recipe for `<input>`, `<textarea>` and `<select>` alike: border, background, radius,
padding, focus ring, placeholder. `.aparte-checkbox`, `.aparte-radio`, `.aparte-switch` and
`.aparte-range` repaint the native controls, so they keep their keyboard behaviour and their form
value.

Around them: `.aparte-field-label`, `.aparte-field-hint`, `.aparte-field-warning`,
`.aparte-field-error`, a `.aparte-field-group` that puts a prefix or a suffix against the field,
and `.aparte-fieldset` for a set of choices.

## Variants

### Sizes, an error, a disabled field

<Variant id="kit-field-states" />

`--sm` and `--lg` follow the [button](/components/button/) sizes, so a field and a button in one
row line up. An error is `aria-invalid` on the field, which draws it, and a message the field
points to.

### Choices as cards

<Variant id="kit-field-choices" />

`.aparte-field-choice--boxed` turns a radio or a checkbox and its text into a card the whole of
which can be clicked.

## Accessibility

- These are native controls: labels, keyboard, form submission and validation work as the browser
  makes them work. Tie each label to its field with `for` and `id`.
- An error is announced through `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` pointing at the message.
- A switch is a checkbox with `role="switch"`.

Most chat products have the same page: the conversations in a column on the left, a bar with the
conversation's title on top, the chat filling the rest. `.aparte-app-shell` is that page as a
grid of two columns and two rows. The sidebar spans both rows; the [header](/components/header/)
and `.aparte-app-shell__main` stack in the second column.

Size the shell (`height: 100dvh` for a whole page) and everything inside follows: the main area
gives the chat the height it needs to scroll. Under 48rem of window, the
[sidebar](/components/sidebar/) leaves the grid and becomes a drawer behind the header's button.
This frame is narrower than that, which is why the sidebar is a drawer here; open the demo on its
own page to see the column.

## Usage

```html
<div class="aparte-app-shell" style="height: 100dvh">
  <aparte-sidebar>…</aparte-sidebar>
  <header class="aparte-app-header">…</header>
  <main class="aparte-app-shell__main">
    <aparte-chat></aparte-chat>
  </main>
</div>
```

The shell is classes and nothing else: no element, no script. The behaviour lives in the parts
that have some: `<aparte-sidebar>` folds and becomes a drawer, `<aparte-split>` drags. The main
area takes a [split](/components/split/) as well as a chat, for a chat beside a preview.

The [Layouts guide](/guides/layout/#the-whole-page-sidebar-header-and-chat) builds a whole page,
and shows the other shapes a chat takes: a reading column, a side panel, a floating composer, a
centred welcome.

## Accessibility

- The shell styles whatever elements carry its classes, so the landmarks are yours to choose: an
  `<aside>` or `<aparte-sidebar>`, a `<header>`, a `<main>` give a screen reader the page's
  regions.

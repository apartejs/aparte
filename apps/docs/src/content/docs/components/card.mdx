`.aparte-card` is a surface with a border and rounded corners, split into an optional
`__header`, a `__body` and an optional `__footer`. It is the natural frame for a
[view of your own](/components/custom-view/) inside a reply: a forecast, an order, a summary.

## Accessibility

- A card is a box, not a landmark: give its title a real heading level when the card is a section
  of the page, and make the whole card a link only when it leads to one place.

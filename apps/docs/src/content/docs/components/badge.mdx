`.aparte-badge` is a status or a count in a pill. It has the [button](/components/button/)'s two
axes: an intent says what it means, a fill (`--solid`, `--soft`, `--outline`) how loudly.
`--sm` and `--lg` size it, and `--dot` is the third form: no label, a coloured dot, always solid.

## Accessibility

- A badge is text, read where it sits. A `--dot` has no text: give it an `aria-label` or a
  visually hidden word, since its colour alone says nothing to someone who cannot see it.

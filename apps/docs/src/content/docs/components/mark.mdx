Among items of the same kind, one is the one a decision landed on: the option picked in a
[select](/components/select/), the conversation open in the
[list](/components/conversation-list/), a question answered in the transcript. The library marks
it the same way everywhere, with a tint of its intent, and `.aparte-mark` puts that tint on an
element of yours.

The intent says what the choice was: primary by default, `--success` for something given or
approved, `--danger` for a destructive outcome, `--neutral` for where you merely are. `--quiet`
is the outcome that did not happen, a declined request: no tint, the muted voice.

## Customize

<Vars />

A bar along the start edge is off by default; `--aparte-mark-bar: 3px` in a theme puts it back,
on every mark at once.

## Accessibility

- The tint is a visual cue. Say the state in the markup as well: `aria-selected` on an option,
  `aria-current` on the item you are in, or the outcome in words.

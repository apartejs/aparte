`.aparte-segmented` is a track of segments with the chosen one raised, the look of
[segmented tabs](/components/tabs/) for a choice rather than a panel. Each segment is a `<label>`
around a native radio, so the group is a real form control: it submits with its form, the arrow
keys move the choice, and a screen reader says "1 of 3".

```html
<div class="aparte-segmented" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Range">
  <label class="aparte-segmented__item">
    <input type="radio" name="range" value="1d" checked>
    <span>1D</span>
  </label>
  <label class="aparte-segmented__item">
    <input type="radio" name="range" value="1m">
    <span>1M</span>
  </label>
</div>
```

Add `aparte-segmented--sm` for a denser row, or `aparte-segmented--block` to fill the line and share
it equally between the segments. The radio stays in the page, invisible and over its segment, so a
click anywhere on the segment lands on it and the focus ring is drawn on the segment.

A row of buttons that each do something, rather than a field of a form, wears the same look:
make each a `<button class="aparte-segmented__item" aria-pressed="true|false">`. The cards' range
buttons are drawn that way.

## Accessibility

- Give the group a name: `role="radiogroup"` and an `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- A label is the segment's accessible name. An icon-only segment needs `aria-label` on its input.
- Focus is visible on the chosen segment, from the keyboard only.

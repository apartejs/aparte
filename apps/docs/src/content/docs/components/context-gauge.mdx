<Variant id="context" />

A model reads a limited number of tokens, and a long conversation reaches the limit before
anyone notices. `<aparte-context>` shows how much is used: the size of the last request, as
the provider reported it, out of the current model's window. The bar takes the width it is
given; `variant="ring"` takes none, for a toolbar, with the full reading in its tooltip.

It needs nothing from your code: it listens to the usage each turn reports, and takes the window
from the model (a provider's model list fills in `contextWindow`) or from the `window`
attribute. Before the first turn, or without a window, it shows nothing.

## Variants

### Warning, then danger

<Variant id="context-levels" />

At 75% of the window the gauge turns `warn`, at 90% `danger` (`warn` and `danger` attributes
move them). It reflects the level in `data-level` and fires `aparte-context-threshold` when it
changes.

## Usage

<Usage />

### When the window is full

With `auto-compact`, a turn that ends in `danger` dispatches `aparte-compact` for its chat, one
request at a time: once it is answered, the next turn still in `danger` asks again. What answers is `@aparte/plugin-compaction`: it summarises the oldest turns and keeps
the recent ones (see [Compaction notice](/components/compaction-notice/)). Without the plugin,
the gauge only measures.

## Accessibility

- The gauge is a `meter` with its minimum, maximum and current value, named from the
  `contextLabel` key ("Context window") followed by the reading.
- The level is written, not only coloured: the reading says how full the window is.

<Variant id="context" />

A model reads a limited number of tokens, and a long conversation reaches the limit before anyone
notices. `<aparte-context>` shows how much is used, out of the current model's window. The bar takes
the width it is given; `variant="ring"` takes none, for a toolbar.

It needs nothing from your code. The reading is the size of the last request as the provider reported
it; when the conversation has changed since — a new message, a summary written, a Restore — it is an
estimate of the next request, prefixed with `~`, calibrated on what the provider reported last. The
window comes from the model (a provider's model list fills in `contextWindow`) or from the `window`
attribute. Before the first turn, or without a window, it shows nothing. A conversation loaded from
storage shows the usage its last reply recorded, and a new chat starts empty.

## What fills the window

Click the reading, or focus it and press Enter, to open what fills the window: the system prompt, the
tools, the summary, the messages, what is free, and "other" — what the provider counted that the page
did not send, such as a server's own instructions. The parts are estimates; the total is the
provider's figure. With [`@aparte/plugin-compaction`](/guides/long-conversations/) set up, the panel
says how much compacting now would free, with **Compact now**; while a compaction runs, and when one
failed, the gauge says so (`data-compact="running"` or `"failed"`), and the panel offers Retry. Escape or
a click outside closes it.

## Variants

### Warning, then danger

<Variant id="context-levels" />

At 75% of the window the gauge turns `warn`, at 90% `danger` (`warn` and `danger` attributes move
them). It reflects the level in `data-level` and fires `aparte-context-threshold` when it changes. A
request the model refused as over its window turns it `danger` until the next reply reports.

## Usage

<Usage />

### When the window is full

The gauge measures; `@aparte/plugin-compaction` decides when to summarise the older turns, past 80 % of
the window by default (see [Long conversations](/guides/long-conversations/)). The `auto-compact`
attribute, which made the gauge ask for a compaction at `danger`, still works but is deprecated: the
plugin compacts on its own once it is set up.

## Accessibility

- The gauge is a `meter` with its minimum, maximum and current value, named from the `contextLabel`
  key ("Context window") followed by the reading.
- The reading is a button with `aria-expanded` that opens the panel; it never opens on hover alone.
- The level is written, not only coloured: the reading says how full the window is.

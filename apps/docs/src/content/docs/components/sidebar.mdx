`<aparte-sidebar>` is the column, with the three behaviours a sidebar needs. It **folds**:
`collapsed` is an attribute you can set, read and save. It becomes a **drawer** on a narrow window.
And its **search** field filters the list of conversations below it as the reader types, hiding a
date group once nothing in it matches.

Its content is yours, in the order you want: a `.aparte-sidebar__header`, a
`.aparte-sidebar__search`, a `.aparte-sidebar__body` holding the
[conversation list](/components/conversation-list/), a `.aparte-sidebar__footer`. The stylesheet
lays them out and draws nothing you did not put there. Without the element, `<aside
class="aparte-sidebar">` is the same look with no behaviour.

## Variants

### As a drawer

<Variant id="sidebar-drawer" />

Under `breakpoint` (48rem of window by default, or `none` for a column that never changes), the
sidebar slides over the page. Open, it dims the page behind a scrim, moves the focus inside,
closes on Escape or a click outside, and gives the focus back to the button that opened it.

In the card of a [floating chat](/components/launcher/#with-a-list-of-conversations), as the
direct child of the card's `.aparte-app-shell`, it is a drawer whatever the window's width,
since the card is narrow in the widest window: it slides over the card, not over the page, and
is a column again once the chat is maximized to the whole window. `breakpoint="none"` keeps a
column there too.

### As a rail

Set `collapsed="rail"` and the column folds to a narrow strip (`--aparte-sidebar-rail-width`, 56px)
instead of to nothing. It stays in the flow and stays usable: it takes no `inert`. What stays is what
you mark `data-rail` among its direct children, an icon-only nav, the toggle, an avatar; the
other children are hidden, and so is any `.aparte-sidebar__label` or `.aparte-sidebar__brand` inside
a marked one. As a drawer a rail means nothing: the drawer is open or off-screen.

```html
<aparte-sidebar collapsed="rail">
  <div class="aparte-sidebar__header" data-rail>
    <button class="aparte-btn aparte-btn--icon" data-aparte-sidebar-toggle aria-label="Menu">
      <aparte-icon name="menu"></aparte-icon>
    </button>
    <span class="aparte-sidebar__brand">Acme</span>
  </div>
  <div class="aparte-sidebar__body">…</div>
</aparte-sidebar>
```

The element does not decide when it is a rail, and has no keyboard shortcut: the toggle you place
sets `collapsed` to `"rail"` or removes it, from your script. Give each icon in a rail its name
with [a tooltip that shows on hover](/components/tooltip/#on-hover-and-focus).

## Usage

<Usage />

Any element carrying `data-aparte-sidebar-toggle` toggles the nearest sidebar, or the one whose id
the attribute names, so a button in the [header](/components/header/) needs no script. One inside a
message's content is ignored: the attribute is for controls your page places, not for a reply.
`aparte-sidebar-toggle` fires on every change, whoever caused it. The search field is any input
with `data-aparte-sidebar-search`; it filters by title, on the rows the list already has, and the
query holds through the list's next renders. When nothing matches, the list says so in a status
line (the locale's `conversationSearchEmpty`), and the list and the sidebar carry
`data-no-match`. `sidebar.filter(query)` does the same from a script and returns how many
conversations match.

## Customize

| Variable | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| `--aparte-sidebar-width` | The column's width, and the drawer's (260px). |
| `--aparte-sidebar-rail-width` | The column folded to a rail, `collapsed="rail"` (56px). |
| `--aparte-sidebar-inset` | The inline inset of its four regions. |
| `--aparte-sidebar-bg` | Its background. |

## Accessibility

- A closed sidebar, folded or slid away, is `inert` and `aria-hidden`: nothing invisible keeps a
  tab stop.
- The toggle's `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` follow the state; the sidebar gives itself an id
  when it has none.
- The open drawer takes the focus and gives it back on close.

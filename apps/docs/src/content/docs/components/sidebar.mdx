`<aparte-sidebar>` is the column, with the three behaviours a sidebar needs. It **folds**:
`collapsed` is an attribute you can set, read and save. It becomes a **drawer** on a narrow window.
And its **search** field filters the list of conversations below it as the reader types, hiding a
date group once nothing in it matches.

Its content is yours, in the order you want: a `.aparte-sidebar__header`, a
`.aparte-sidebar__search`, a `.aparte-sidebar__body` holding the
[conversation list](/components/conversation-list/), a `.aparte-sidebar__footer`. The stylesheet
lays them out and draws nothing you did not put there. Without the element, `<aside
class="aparte-sidebar">` is the same look with no behaviour.

## Variants

### As a drawer

<Variant id="sidebar-drawer" />

Under `breakpoint` (48rem of window by default, or `none` for a column that never changes), the
sidebar slides over the page. Open, it dims the page behind a scrim, moves the focus inside,
closes on Escape or a click outside, and gives the focus back to the button that opened it.

## Usage

<Usage />

Any element carrying `data-aparte-sidebar-toggle` toggles the nearest sidebar, or the one whose id
the attribute names, so a button in the [header](/components/header/) needs no script.
`aparte-sidebar-toggle` fires on every change, whoever caused it. The search field is any input
with `data-aparte-sidebar-search`; it filters by title, on the rows the list already has.

## Customize

| Variable | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| `--aparte-sidebar-width` | The column's width, and the drawer's (260px). |
| `--aparte-sidebar-inset` | The inline inset of its four regions. |
| `--aparte-sidebar-bg` | Its background. |

## Accessibility

- A closed sidebar, folded or slid away, is `inert` and `aria-hidden`: nothing invisible keeps a
  tab stop.
- The toggle's `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` follow the state; the sidebar gives itself an id
  when it has none.
- The open drawer takes the focus and gives it back on close.

`.aparte-popover` is deliberately just a box: the surface, border and shadow that say "this
floats above the page". What goes inside (a form, a colour picker, a card of details) and where
the box sits are yours. Its one limit is `--aparte-popover-max-width`, so that its content cannot
grow it without bound.

The browser's own `popover` attribute pairs well with it: `popover` on the box and
`popovertarget` on its button open and close it with no script, above everything, and close it on
Escape or a click outside.

## Accessibility

- A popover holding a form is a non-modal dialog: give it `role="dialog"` and a name
  (`aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`), and move the focus into it when it opens.

`.aparte-tooltip` is a short label in a dark chip with an arrow. Its colours are fixed rather
than themed, on purpose: it has to stay legible over whatever it covers, a bright image or a dark
code block, in either theme.

Wrap the trigger and the tooltip in `.aparte-tooltip-anchor` and set `data-side` (`top`,
`bottom`, `start` or `end`): the tooltip sits on that side, `--aparte-tooltip-gap` away, with its
arrow turned to match. Showing it on hover and focus is your script's or your CSS's, and so is
keeping it on screen near an edge. It never catches the pointer itself.

## Accessibility

- Give the tooltip `role="tooltip"` and an id, and point the trigger at it with
  `aria-describedby`; show it on focus as well as on hover, and hide it on Escape.
- A tooltip is a hint: anything the reader must know belongs in the page.

<Variant id="chat" />

`<aparte-chat>` lays its children out as a column: the transcript takes the space left and
scrolls, the composer keeps its own height below it. Left empty, it composes itself — a
transcript, the panel where questions and approvals appear, and a composer with a field and a
send button. Put an `<aparte-chat-viewport>` inside and it uses your children as they are.

The tag draws the chat; something has to answer it. `AparteClient` listens for the messages it
sends and streams the replies back (see [Getting started](/guides/getting-started/)).

## Variants

### With a conversation

<Variant id="chat-conversation" />

`chat.viewport` is the transcript. Restoring a conversation is appending its messages; the
client does the same during a turn.

### The composer floating over the transcript

<Variant id="chat-overlay" />

`overlay-composer` lets the transcript scroll under the composer, which floats at the bottom:
the layout of ChatGPT, where the scrollbar runs the whole height of the column.

### Composed by hand

<Variant id="chat-composed" />

Anything you add is another row of the column, in DOM order: a header, a banner above the
composer. `center-empty` keeps the composer centred as a welcome screen until the first message
lands, then slides it down; `attachments` adds the file picker to the composed composer.

## Accessibility

- The transcript is a named, focusable `role="log"`, so a screen reader reaches the
  conversation with the keyboard and hears new messages.
- Everything inside keeps its own semantics: see [Conversation](/components/conversation/),
  [Message](/components/message/) and [Composer](/components/composer/).

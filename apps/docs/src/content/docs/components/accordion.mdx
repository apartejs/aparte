A column of items, each a header and the panel it reveals. The header can be a native `<summary>`
inside `<details>`, and then the browser opens and closes it, or a `<div role="button"
aria-expanded>`, and then your code does. The chevron turns when the item is open, either way.
The chat's [reasoning](/components/reasoning/) block is an accordion item on `<details>`.

## Accessibility

- `<details>` and `<summary>` need nothing more: they are keyboard-operable and announce whether
  they are open.
- With `role="button"`, keep `aria-expanded` in step with the panel, and make the header
  focusable with `tabindex="0"` and Enter or Space.

`<aparte-chat-viewport>` is the conversation as a list of messages. It renders a bubble for each,
keeps the view at the bottom while new text arrives, and stops following as soon as the reader
scrolls up; a button brings them back. It also holds the branches a retry creates, so moving
between two versions of a reply is the viewport re-rendering a different path.

Inside an [`<aparte-chat>`](/components/chat/) it is `chat.viewport`. On its own, it is the
transcript of a page that lays out the rest itself.

## Variants

### A reply streaming in

<Variant id="conversation-streaming" />

A turn is `appendMessage()`, then `appendToken()` for each chunk, then `completeMessage()`.
`AparteClient` does exactly this for you; drive it by hand when your own code runs the loop.

### While the messages load

<Variant id="conversation-loading" />

`loading` says a conversation was chosen and its messages are on their way: the viewport shows
the outline of a conversation rather than an empty column, and says so to a screen reader.

## Accessibility

- The transcript is a focusable `role="log"`, named by the `transcript` locale key.
- While `loading`, it is `aria-busy` and a visually hidden line names the wait, because screen
  readers ignore `aria-busy` alone.
- The scroll-to-bottom button is named by the `scrollToBottom` locale key.

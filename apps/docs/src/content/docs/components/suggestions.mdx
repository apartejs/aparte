An empty chat is a blank page, and three good starters tell a reader what it is for. Each one
is a button: a click puts its prompt in the composer and sends it. With `empty-only`, the row
is there while the conversation is empty and gone after the first message. Try one above:
the scripted model answers.

The click goes through the composer, as if the prompt had been typed, so it obeys the same
rules: nothing is sent while the composer is disabled, a reply is streaming, or no model is
selected.

## Variants

### Fill without sending

<Variant id="suggestions-fill" />

`mode="fill"` puts the prompt in the input and focuses it, for prompts the reader is meant to
finish or adjust.

## Usage

Place it inside the [composer](/components/composer/), before its shell. A suggestion is a
string, or a `{ label, prompt }` pair when the button's text and the prompt sent differ. The
`suggestions` attribute takes them as JSON; the property takes the array.

<Usage />

To act on a click yourself, listen for `aparte-suggestion`: it fires first and is cancelable,
and `preventDefault()` leaves the composer untouched.

## Customize

The row has no variables of its own: its spacing comes from the `--aparte-space-*` scale and
its buttons wear the [button](/components/button/) recipe, so it follows your theme.

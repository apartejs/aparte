`.aparte-progress` is the track and `.aparte-progress__bar` the part that fills, to a width you
set. `--indeterminate` moves the bar across when you cannot say how far along the wait is. The
[context gauge](/components/context-gauge/) is drawn with this recipe.

## Accessibility

- The recipe paints; the meaning is yours to give. A bar with a value takes
  `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`, and a name
  saying what is progressing.
- The bar is drawn with a border, so it stays visible in forced-colours mode.

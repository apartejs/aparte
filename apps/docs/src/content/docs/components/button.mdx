`.aparte-btn` on a `<button>` gives it the look of the chat's own buttons: the send button, the
copy button, the approval options. Two axes make every button. An **intent** says what the
colour means (primary, neutral, success, warning, danger, info, secondary); a **fill** says how
loudly (solid, soft, outline, surface, or none, which is the quiet ghost a chat uses most). Any
intent works with any fill.

`.aparte-btn-group` fuses neighbouring buttons into one control, the way the `‹ 1/2 ›` picker
and the site's own Preview/Code switch are drawn.

`--icon` makes a square button for a lone glyph, `--circle` a round one, `--pill` rounds the
ends, `--block` takes the full width.

## Accessibility

- Use a real `<button>` (or an `<a>` for a link): the classes give the look, the element gives
  the keyboard and the role.
- An icon-only button needs an `aria-label`; the glyph inside is hidden from screen readers.
- A group of buttons that acts as one control takes `role="group"` and a label.

The 25 built-in glyphs are redrawn — same names, same 24-grid conventions and optical weight, our own path data. Nothing to change in your code; an icon provider you registered is unaffected.

The previous set carried Feather's and Lucide's coordinates verbatim under a header saying nothing was imported from them — true at runtime, irrelevant in license terms. Redrawing is the cleaner answer than carrying their notices: no set to credit, nothing to keep in step. The opt-in extended set (`@aparte/core/icons`) is next on the same list.

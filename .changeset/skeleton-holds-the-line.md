A skeleton text line holds the place of the line it stands for: its height is the content's font size and its gap the rest of the content line (`--aparte-skeleton-text-height`, `--aparte-skeleton-text-gap` are derived from `--aparte-content-font-size` and `--aparte-content-line-height`).

The bar was 12px on an 18px step while a line of content is 16px on a 27px step, so the text that replaced it jumped by a third of a line per line. A placeholder that does not hold the place it promises is not a placeholder.
